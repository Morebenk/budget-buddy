How it works

  1. Say what you want to splitIntroduce yourself and pick the services you care about. You get pinged when a seat opens.
  2. Browse the boardEvery offer and request is one tagged post. Filter by service, or by what's still open.
  3. Agree before money movesPrice per seat, renewal date, payment method. Check the other person's vouches first.
  4. Vouch afterwardsOnce a split has run a month, say so. That record is the only trust this community has.

The one rule that matters

We split plans. We never share logins.

A legitimate family or duo plan lets the holder invite you on as a member. You get your own login, your own profile, your own library. Nobody hands over a password.

If someone asks for your password, or offers to sell you theirs, they are not splitting a plan with you — they are handing over access to an account that holds their payment method and personal data, or asking you to do the same. That is against our rules and it gets you removed.
