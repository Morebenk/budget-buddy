How it works

Four steps, and the whole thing happens between you and the person you split with.

  1. Say what you wantPick your services when you join. You get pinged the moment a seat opens on one of them.
  2. Browse the boardEvery offer and request is one tagged post. Filter by service, or by what's still open.
  3. Agree the detailsPrice per seat, renewal date, how you'll pay. Check their vouches before any money moves.
  4. Vouch afterwardsAfter a month, say how it went. That record is what makes the next split safe for someone else.

Splitting without getting burned

How you and your group run the plan is between you. These are the things people wish they'd done before the first payment — not rules, just what goes wrong when nobody agrees it up front.

✓ Worth sorting first

  • Turn on 2FA on the account — without it, anyone in the group can reset the password and lock everyone else out
  • Use a password that isn't reused anywhere else
  • Agree who holds the plan, what each person pays and when it renews
  • Start with one month. Run it once before committing to a year
  • Check their vouches — nobody can vouch for a split that hasn't actually run

✗ Not what this place is for

  • Buying or selling accounts. This is people splitting a bill, not a shop
  • Reselling seats at a markup, bulk accounts, "lifetime" offers
  • Shop links and invites to other servers
  • Asking anyone for identity documents

Questions people actually ask

How does a split actually work?

One person holds the plan and pays the provider. Everyone else pays them their share. How you run it — who holds it, how people get access, how you collect the money — is for your group to agree between yourselves. Different services work differently, so there's no single answer that fits all of them.

How do I know I won't get scammed?

You don't, on day one — which is exactly why vouches exist. Nobody can vouch for a split that hasn't actually run for a month, so the record builds slowly and honestly. Check it before you send anyone money, agree the renewal date up front, and start with one month rather than a year.

Do you take a cut?

No. There's no fee to join, no fee to offer a seat, and no payment passes through us. You arrange everything directly with the people you split with. That's the whole reason this is a Discord community and not a business.

What if I just want to lurk?

Fine. Join, pick the services you care about, and you'll get pinged when a seat opens on one of them. Plenty of people sit quietly until the right plan shows up.

Which services can I split?

Music, streaming, VPNs, gaming and AI tools — around fourteen categories on the board, plus an Other tag for anything that doesn't fit. The busiest are Spotify, ExpressVPN and Netflix.
